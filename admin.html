Day 5: The Traitor

Make sure Day 4 is closed so every finalist is on Day 5, then press the button. It forms groups of about 4 from the active players on Day 5, picks one secret traitor per group and starts after the lobby. Each round has a chat discussion and then a vote. Total time = rounds x (discussion + vote).

Day 6: Sacrifices

Solo game. Each player gets their own clock from the moment they press Start. At the end of the day (or when you press "Close day now") finishers are ranked by a hidden score and a percentage is eliminated. Anyone who did not finish is eliminated. Players are told nothing until the day closes.

Day 7 - Dark Maze (hunters vs survivors) + Tightrope

Press "Create rooms and open the maze" when the players are online. Each room gets the same number of hunters and survivors. Hunters try to catch survivors (a catch sends the survivor back to the start and moves the hunter to a random place, and hunters cannot enter the survivors' safe zone). Survivors who reach the exit go on to the rope. An odd player out watches the rooms and goes to the rope when the maze ends. Survivors who never escape are eliminated; hunters pass if enough survivors in their room failed and they made enough kills. The rope then ranks everyone by time.
